CutList Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

CutList Pro is a woodworking cut-list optimizer that runs entirely on your device. We designed it to be privacy-friendly by default.

Data we collect

None. CutList Pro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no account, no login, and no sign-up.

Your projects

All the data you create — projects, parts, dimensions, and cut plans — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). It never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it (for example, saving or sharing a PDF or text cut plan using the iOS share sheet).

Network & tracking

CutList Pro works fully offline. It contains no ads, no analytics, no third-party tracking SDKs, and does not make network requests to us.

In-app feedback link

The app includes an optional "Feedback" link that opens an external Google Form in your browser. If you choose to use it, any information you submit there is handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy — not by CutList Pro. The app itself still collects and transmits no data.

App Store

Apple may collect standard purchase and diagnostic information as described in Apple's own privacy policy. This is handled by Apple, not by CutList Pro.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
